The Problem

🏭

Medical Device Assembly

A catheter tube is inserted into a medical port. The insertion depth must be 13–17 mm. Any unit outside this range is defective.

⚠️

The Failure Pattern

Assembly lines drift over time due to material variation (bag port diameter, tube OD) and servo de-calibration. A gradual trend leads to the first failing unit — but it is predictable.

🎯

The Challenge

  • Monitor 3 assembly lines simultaneously in real time
  • Detect drift before a unit goes out of spec
  • Predict the exact unit number of the first failure (X)
  • Lock the prediction early — cannot be changed
  • Prove the evidence was not tampered with

client.py

HTTP with 4 retries. Accepts list, wrapped list, or single-object API responses — tolerant of shape changes.

engine.py

Orchestrates everything: polling loop, writes journal (fsync) before DB, runs model, applies lock rules, evaluates E & L.

store.py

Append-only SQLite. SHA-256 per record. Hash chain across all tables. Triggers block UPDATE/DELETE at the database engine level.

model.py

Pure functions. EWMA alarm on axis error. CUSUM alarm on depth. Ridge regression forecast with Gate 2 prior (+0.001 in clearance → +0.76 mm depth).

Prediction Model

Step 1 — De-calibration Alarm

  • EWMA on axis following error → detects servo drift
  • CUSUM on insertion depth → detects sustained trend toward spec limit
  • Need ≥ 30 units before forecasting starts
  • Both alarms can fire independently

Step 2 — Ridge Regression Forecast

  • Features: bag/tube clearance, axis error, pressure, speed
  • Gate 2 prior: +0.001 in clearance → +0.76 mm depth
  • Recency-weighted linear trend on explained signal
  • Extrapolated with uncertainty band

Step 3 — First-Failure Distribution

  • P(failure) per future unit = P(depth outside 13–17mm)
  • f(k) = P_k × ∏(1 − P_j) for j < k
  • Median of distribution = predicted unit X
